The people behind Think Through

A small, hands-on studio built on one idea — every business deserves a website that actually works for them, not a template forced onto their brand.

LEADERSHIP

Founded by two people who'd rather ship something real than talk about it.

Mahesh Kumar Selvam

Founder

Mahesh leads design and engineering at Think Through Technology. With a background spanning QA automation and full-stack freelance development, he's shipped 30+ business websites across real estate, hospitality, healthcare and construction — each one built single-file, mobile-first, and wired to run itself with Firebase and WhatsApp automation. He's also the one who insists on testing everything twice before it ships.

Sajitha Vijayakumar

Co-Founder

Sajitha co-founded Think Through Technology to bring structure and client-first thinking to every project — from first conversation to final handover. She works closely on client relationships, project scoping and quality, making sure every build stays true to what the business actually needs, not just what looks good in a demo.

Built for the Business, Not the Portfolio

Every design decision is judged by one question: does this help the client's business grow?

Fast, Lightweight, Reliable

No bloated frameworks. Sites are lean, load fast on mobile, and rarely break.

Straight Talk, Real Timelines

We tell clients what's actually possible — and then we deliver on it, on schedule.

Think Through Technology started the way most good studios do — with one client, one deadline, and a lot of late nights. What began as freelance website builds for local Chennai businesses grew into a full studio as word spread: ambulance services, real estate agencies, resorts, barbershops and construction firms all needed the same thing — a site that looked professional, worked on a phone, and didn't need a developer on call to keep running.

Today the studio is expanding beyond static websites into CRM software, automation, and digital marketing — but the philosophy hasn't changed. Think it through, build it right, ship it fast.
