Service Delivery Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Think Through Technology provides digital services: website design and development, software, automation and related online services. We do not sell or ship any physical products, so no shipping or courier charges apply.

1. How services are delivered

  • Websites are delivered as a live link on your domain, or on a hosting or preview link before go-live.
  • Software, CRM and admin dashboards are delivered as live web applications with login access for you and your team.
  • Source files, credentials and documentation, where included in the quotation, are shared by email, WhatsApp, Google Drive or a GitHub repository.

2. Delivery timelines

  • The timeline for each project is stated in the quotation and starts after we receive the advance payment and the content needed to begin.
  • As a guide: single-page or landing-page websites usually take 3–7 working days; multi-page business websites 7–20 working days; CRM, e-commerce and custom software as per the agreed project plan.
  • Timelines may be extended if content, feedback or payments are delayed, or if the scope changes.

3. Confirmation of payment and start of work

Once your online payment is successful, you will receive a payment confirmation from Cashfree Payments by SMS or email, and we will confirm receipt and the next steps on WhatsApp or email within 1 working day.

4. Handover

On completion and receipt of full payment, we hand over the live website or application along with any credentials and files included in your quotation.

5. Delivery issues

If you have not received a deliverable you paid for, or cannot access it, please contact us and we will resolve it promptly.
