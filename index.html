How a project goes

  1. Tell us about the business

    A WhatsApp message or a call is enough. We ask what you sell, who buys it and what the site has to do for you.

  2. Get a written quote

    What we'll build and what it costs, on paper, before any work starts.

  3. See it working

    You get a link to the actual site on your own phone, not a picture of one. Tell us what to change and we change it.

  4. Go live on your domain

    We connect your domain, set the site up for Google search and show you how to update it.
