Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Think Through Technology ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you visit thinkthroughtechnology.in (operated by Mahesh Kumar Selvam, proprietor of Think Through Technology), contact us, or pay us for our services. By using this website or our services, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, business name, phone number, email address and project details when you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, email or any form on this website.
  • Project material: content you share for your website or software, such as text, images, logos and login details for domains, hosting or third-party services.
  • Payment information: when you pay us online, the payment is processed by our payment partner, Cashfree Payments. We receive only the transaction details (name, phone, email, amount, payment status and reference ID). We never receive or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net banking password.
  • Usage information: basic, non-identifying information such as browser type, device and pages visited, which may be collected through standard server logs or analytics tools.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotations.
  • To design, build, deliver and maintain the websites and software you order.
  • To send invoices and payment links, confirm payments and issue receipts.
  • To provide support and communicate about your project.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

3. Sharing of information

We share information only when necessary:

  • Payment processing: with Cashfree Payments and banks/UPI networks to process your payment securely.
  • Service providers: with hosting, domain, email and cloud providers we use to deliver your project (for example, Google Firebase, GitHub or Vercel), only as needed to provide the service.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, court order or a government authority.

4. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption on this website and access-restricted accounts. Online payments are handled on Cashfree's secure, PCI-DSS compliant checkout. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take care to protect your data.

5. Cookies

This website may use small files stored in your browser (cookies or local storage) to remember preferences such as day/night mode. You can clear or block these in your browser settings.

6. Data retention

We keep project and payment records for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal and tax requirements. You may ask us to delete information that we are not legally required to keep.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Third-party links

Our website may link to other websites, including client websites in our portfolio. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
