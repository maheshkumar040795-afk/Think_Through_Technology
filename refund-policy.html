Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for services purchased from Think Through Technology. Because our services are custom design and development work, refunds depend on how much work has been completed.

1. Cancellation by the Client

  • You may cancel a project by informing us in writing by email or WhatsApp.
  • Before work starts: if you cancel within 48 hours of paying the advance and before any work has started, you will receive a full refund of the advance.
  • After work has started: the advance covers planning, design and development time already spent. We will calculate the value of the work completed up to the cancellation date and refund any unused balance of the amount paid, if any.
  • After approval or delivery: once a design, milestone or the final project has been approved or delivered, the payment for that stage is non-refundable.

2. Non-refundable items

  • Domain registrations and renewals.
  • Hosting, cloud, email, SMS/WhatsApp API or other third-party charges already paid on your behalf.
  • Paid themes, plugins, stock images or licences purchased for your project.
  • Monthly or annual maintenance for a period that has already started.

3. Cancellation by us

If we are unable to start or complete your project for reasons on our side, we will refund the amount paid for work not delivered.

4. Duplicate or failed payments

  • If you were charged twice for the same payment, or charged more than the agreed amount, the extra amount will be refunded in full after verification.
  • If money was debited but the payment failed, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank or payment provider within 5–7 working days. If it is not, contact us with the transaction reference and we will help resolve it with Cashfree Payments.

5. How to request a refund

Email thinkthroughtechnology@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 80153 04105 with your name, invoice or quote number, payment date, amount and transaction reference, and the reason for the request. We will reply within 3 working days.

6. Refund timeline

Approved refunds are initiated within 7 working days to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet). Depending on your bank, the amount may take a further 5–7 working days to appear in your account.
